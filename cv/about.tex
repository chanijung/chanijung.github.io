Hi! I'm a recent master's graduate in \href{https://cs.kaist.ac.kr}{School of Computing at KAIST}, where I was advised by \href{https://aliceoh9.github.io}{Alice Oh}. I'm currently collaborating with  \href{https://lti.cs.cmu.edu}{Language Technologies Institute at CMU}, working with \href{https://maartensap.com}{Maarten Sap}.

My research interests lie in human-centered NLP. I have focused on key yet under-explored facets of socio-cultural intelligence in language models, including cultural sensitivity, theory of mind, and question-asking abilities.
